    Field notes

    How the feeder gets to your screen

    1. A camera at the feeder

      A weatherproof camera watches the platform feeder from a few feet away — close enough to count tail feathers.

    2. The good bits, trimmed

      Most footage is an empty feeder. The best visits are trimmed into short clips and tagged by species.

    3. Delivered from the cloud

      Clips live in cloud storage and stream to you from a nearby edge server, so a popular video never slows the site.

    Support the archive
